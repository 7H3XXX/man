## Rules

Two players, one keyboard and 60 seconds. Each player can move, jump and attack. When a fighter runs out of health or the timer hits zero, the game declares the winner, or a tie if both fighters have the same health.

### How to play
Player 1 moves with A and D, jumps with W and attacks with Space.<br/>
Player 2 moves with the left and right arrows, jumps with the up arrow and attacks with the down arrow.

## Craft

No engine, no library, just a canvas of and JavaScript. Sounds simple ? Well it's not, but it's the best way to understand how a game really works.

**Game loop** - Everything happens inside `requestAnimationFrame`. Each frame clears the canvas and draws the background, the animated shop and both fighters again.

**Gravity** - A constant value pulls the fighters down every frame until they touch the ground, that's what gives jumps their natural arc.

**Sprites** - A `Sprite` class plays the sprite sheets frame by frame, and a `Fighter` class extends it with seven states: idle, run, jump, fall, attack, take hit and death. Each state swaps the sprite sheet so the character always looks like what he's doing.

**Collisions** - Each fighter has an attack box. A hit only counts when this box overlaps the opponent's body during an attack, a simple rectangle on rectangle check.

**Health and timer** - Health bars and the countdown live in the DOM above the canvas, and the game ends as soon as one of them runs out.

## What I learned

After writing the loop, the gravity and the collisions myself, game engines finally made sense. I now understand web games work behind the scenes, how to structure them, and bit more about physics and game loops.
