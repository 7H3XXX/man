The website had to make a serious first impression, explain what the studio does without jargon and make booking a call the obvious next step on every screen.

## Concept

The page follows the questions a decision maker asks, in the order he asks them. What do you do? Who do you do it for? Have you done it before? How do you work? How do I reach you?

So the home page is organized like this:
- The hero, showing what the studio does and a call-to-action to Book a call
- The studio and its mission
- Selected work, with a case study for each
- What we do, presenting their services
- How we work, their five steps framework (Audit, Diagnose, Design, Build, Accelerate)
- Book a call, a form to get precise details on leads and start the conversation

## Craft

### Art direction

Big serif headlines, generous white space, dark tones and iridescent 3D images. We needed a premium and technical feel at the same time, without falling into the common 😏.

<Image src="/images/projects/indomptable-studio/rings.webp" width="672" height="120" alt="Iridescent glass rings stacked in a curve, the hero image of the studio website"/>

### Decisions

The website is built with Next.js, Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui on top of [Enterprise UI](/projects/enterprise-ui/case-study) blocks.

**First impression** - Decision makers form an opinion in seconds. The hero text shows up right away, introducing the studio and how it can help them.

**Credibility** - A studio that sells transformation has to show proof. Each project gets a full case study, so a buyer sees how the team thinks before the first call.

**Conversion** - Long forms scare people away. I went for step form with pinpoint questions, and the Book a call button stays in the header on every page.

**Visibility** - Buyers often find a studio through search or a shared link. Every case study has its own share image, structured data and canonical URL, so it looks right wherever it shows up.

**Accessibility** - Navigating through the website can be done with keys, the website passes most accessibility checks.

## Result

The studio now has a website that looks like the work it sells, with a clear story for buyers, proof in every case study and one obvious next step on every screen.
